# LicenseTrack – Legal pages

Public pages for **LicenseTrack**, an offline-first Android app for tracking business licences, renewal dates and documents.

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) | What the app does and does not do with your information |
| [Terms of Use](terms.html) | Rules for using the app, plans and purchases |

These pages are published with GitHub Pages and linked from the app and from the Google Play listing.

## Summary

- LicenseTrack works without an account. Your records stay on your device.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Purchases are handled by Google Play.
