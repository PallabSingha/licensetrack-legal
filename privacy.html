LicenseTrack – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

LicenseTrack is an offline-first Android app that helps you track business licences, renewal dates and documents. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

1. Your records stay on your device

LicenseTrack does not need an account. The businesses, licences, dates, notes, renewal history and attached documents you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer does not receive them.

2. What leaves your device, and only when you choose

Reports, attachments and backup files are shared or saved only when you tap Share, Save or Create backup, to the app or folder you pick. Backup files can be protected with a password. Without a password, anyone who has the file can read it.

3. Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. LicenseTrack asks Google Play which products you own and keeps a small record of the answer (a product name, a short reference to the purchase and the time it was confirmed) so Premium keeps working offline for a limited time. We never see or store your payment details.

4. App lock

If you turn on app lock, Android asks for your phone's PIN, pattern, password or biometrics. LicenseTrack never receives or stores them.

5. Notifications

Reminders are created on your device from the dates you enter. They are not sent from any server.

6. No analytics or ads

LicenseTrack contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries and does not upload crash reports. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

7. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, plus the app version and device model that the email template adds. Please do not send licence documents unless we ask and you agree. We use this only to answer you.

8. Deleting your data

Use Backup & restore → Delete all local data inside the app, or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

9. Children

LicenseTrack is intended for business users and is not directed at children.
